The district itself

上海市静安区 · central Shanghai · the only one of the four districts that approached Brisen first

How they describe themselves

Their own self-introduction, sent through CITIC Europe on 7 August 2026

Located in the heart of Shanghai, Jing'an District gets its name from the ancient Jing'an Temple. It is a vibrant blend of old and new — home to luxury shopping streets like West Nanjing Road, modern tech hubs, and charming traditional neighborhoods… landmarks range from the golden-roofed Jing'an Temple and historic shikumen lane houses at Zhang Yuan, to fairytale-like European villas (Mahlers Hall) and key WWII sites like the Sihang Warehouse.

They led with historic-fabric regeneration and luxury retail, not with technology. Worth remembering: it is the closest of the four districts to what Brisen actually does.

What the district is, in its own policy words

A headquarters-and-data district. Its 2026 business-environment plan builds a headquarters-economy centre, runs a "headquarters upgrading" programme, chases multinational regional headquarters, functional global divisions and treasury centres, and establishes a district cross-border data service centre.

Its named industry tracks: beauty and health · ultra-high-definition AI audiovisual and cultural-creative · blockchain · data intelligence · headquarters economy.

Reported, not verified this pass: 142 cumulative multinational regional headquarters, about 11% of Shanghai's total, 54 of them added since the start of the 14th Five-Year Plan.

The money question

Read in full: two district policies and the one 2026 fund that carries figures

What Jing'an publishes for an inbound foreign investor No figure exists

Its 2024 measures on building a modern industrial system explicitly back multinational regional headquarters and R&D centres — but the text carries no cash figure anywhere. It sets direction and delegates to the commerce commission, the science-and-economy commission and the promotion office.

Its 2026 business-environment action plan builds the headquarters-economy centre, the cross-border data service centre, the 静安国际会客厅 one-stop window and a QFLP route — again, no published number.

So the absence is real, but it is not the same as an absence of support. Jing'an's route is the international window plus municipal headquarters recognition, both discretionary.

The one fund with numbers points outward, not inward

2026 Jing'an special fund for building a going-abroad service platform, first batch, 23 April 2026

  • Up to RMB 5 million for a newly established going-abroad headquarters in Jing'an, or an existing one upgraded to Asia-Pacific or global
  • RMB 500,000 for an overseas operating entity
  • Overseas investment or M&A: up to 20% of repatriated dividends, max RMB 5 million per company per year
  • International market development: up to 50%, max RMB 2 million a year, plus a RMB 300,000 first-landing award
  • Going-out professional services: up to 20%, max RMB 2 million per project, RMB 10 million per company a year
  • Platform building up to 50%, max RMB 500,000 · demonstration projects RMB 200,000

None of it is available to an inbound foreign investor. It requires establishment in Jing'an, sound operations, no serious violations and a demonstrable overseas footprint.

Their three showpieces

What they will walk a visitor through, and what each one actually is

张园 Zhang Yuan — the urban-renewal flagship

Founded 1882, called "the finest garden in Shanghai"; the largest and best-preserved shikumen complex surviving in the city, and the central city's first retain-renovate-demolish renewal pilot. West zone reopened 2022; the east zone's first phase opened June 2026. Operated in its entirety by Swire Properties, with Louis Vuitton and Piaget among the first-tier brands in occupation.

This is the stop closest to Brisen's own trade. If you want one substantive exchange on the call, heritage-led mixed-use regeneration is it.

静安国际会客厅 Jing'an Global Connect — the one-stop window

At 南京西路1600号, the former city air terminal, roughly 5,000 sqm, where three metro lines meet; formally opened September 2025. Services: whole-chain support for enterprises going abroad, whole-process cross-border data services, and convenience services for foreign nationals — foreign-related notarisation, 24-hour smart Hong Kong/Macau endorsement, foreign-related marriage registration. In the 2026 plan it is the "1+3+N" internationalisation window.

美创静界 — the beauty and health incubator

A specialist incubator on a government-plus-medical-research-institution-plus-enterprise model, integrating the dermatology department of Fudan University's Huashan Hospital and the Shanghai Dermatology Hospital, with shared public laboratories for concept validation, efficacy testing and sensory/formulation work. Launched May 2025; more than 130 enterprises at over 90% occupancy.
