A whole fleet already running in a hotel 30 minutes from yours

Shanghai company, founded 2010 · the fleet is at the Traders / Shangri-La Hongqiao

What is actually running there: a lobby humanoid (XMAN-R1), room delivery (W3), luggage (S100), cleaning (C40) and food service (T10 and T3) — 8 machines working together in one property. 6 — Yunji 云迹 A hotel company that happens to make robots

Sells the operating system, not the machine

Hong Kong listed · filing of 29 April 2026

Hotels were about 81% of its 2025 revenue. It claims the largest share in China for hotel service robots and hotel AI agents, with Huazhu and Jinjiang among its groups. What it sells is a hotel digital operating system — voice, work orders, robot dispatch — with delivery machines on top.

Why that matters to you: every other company on this page sells you a machine. This one sells the layer that makes a mixed fleet behave as one system, which is the thing you cannot buy from a robot maker.

1 — Alibaba, Qwen 通义 Ask first

The only lab with a working hotel stack behind it

徐汇滨江 West Bund · D-block 龙启路 8号 · X-block 龙耀路 8号 · Hangzhou team at Xixi, 969 West Wen Yi Road

Licence: Qwen3 and Qwen3.8-27B are Apache 2.0 — run them anywhere, including on your own European machines. Do not ship Qwen3.8-Max. Its own licence adds a separate-licence gate above 50 million dollars of trailing revenue for model-as-a-service and coding assistants, and a name-in-the-interface duty above 20 million dollars monthly. A hotel concierge probably sits in the carve-out — but that is not advice, and the open models are better anyway.

Hotel evidence, real but not luxury: Dossen 东呈 runs Qwen across 3,500+ midscale hotels with a booking assistant and an AI hub. Fosun Tourism and Club Med run an agent on Qwen. Tongcheng's 旅智云 property-management system plugged both Qwen and DeepSeek. None of them is a Mandarin Oriental — they prove the stack exists, not that anyone luxury has done it.

Note: the printed 18 September Pudong programme names Alibaba Wuying, not the Qwen team.

The ask: a 14–16 September introduction at the Xuhui West Bund campus, with Alibaba Cloud or Tongyi people who can talk about self-hosting on NVIDIA — not the China API. Hangzhou instead means a full day gone.

4 — MiniMax Only for video and speech

The one with a real licence tether

11/F, Building B, Xinyan Mansion, 桂箐路 65号, Xuhui · no visitor programme found

Licence: a community licence, not open source — do not call it open. Commercial use requires showing "Built with MiniMax" and, above 20 million dollars of yearly product revenue, prior written authorisation. Fine-tunes and hosted versions count as commercial use. A Brisen hotel product sits above that line, so the tether is real.

What is actually useful: Hailuo for video and their speech models. Their long-context model is the pitch for long guest history. Their hosted service processes in China unless a data agreement names another region.

The ask, if you make it: "show us video and speech, and explain how the 20-million authorisation works for a European hotel."
